📘 Artifact Set A: The Owner's Manual (Layman's Guide)
Project Name: The "Wise" Adaptive Trading Platform Core Philosophy: "Conservative Aggression" — Aggressively hunt for profit, conservatively manage risk. Never incur a total loss.

1. How It Works (The Anatomy)
Think of this system not as a computer program, but as a Digital Employee with four distinct parts:

The Brain (State Manager): This is the central nervous system. It holds the current "truth" of the market. It is fast, efficient, and connects everything.

The Eyes (External Intelligence): It reads news, checks sentiment, and looks at "life events" (earnings, wars, rate cuts) to tell the Brain if the market is safe or dangerous.

The Memory (Wisdom Builder): This is the unique feature. Every night, it reviews every trade. If it lost money, it asks "Why?" and adjusts its confidence. It learns like a human, getting smarter over time.

The Hands (Execution): This part places the trades. It has a "tremor filter" (Circuit Breaker). If the system tries to lose too much money, the Hands physically lock up and refuse to trade.

2. The Strategies (How It Makes Money)
Mean Reversion (The Rubber Band): Buys when a stock is stretched too far down, expecting it to snap back.

Trend Following (The Surfer): Spots a wave (trend) forming and rides it until it breaks.

Stat Arb (The Twin): Looks at two related stocks (like Coke vs. Pepsi). If they drift apart, it bets they will come back together.

Options Specialist: Takes quick profits. It doesn't wait for the jackpot; it takes the money off the table when the math says "good enough."

3. Why It Is Safe
The Circuit Breaker: A hard-coded safety switch. If daily losses hit $500 (or your set limit), the system shuts down. No AI hallucination can override this.

The "Tremor" Filter: It ignores "noisy" data. It waits for clear, strong signals before acting.
