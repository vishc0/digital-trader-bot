# Skillsets, Roles, and Responsibilities

## Required skillsets

- Trading domain modeling
- Broker integration engineering
- Portfolio accounting and P&L analytics
- Risk and compliance engineering
- AI prompt and evaluation design
- Platform architecture and SRE
- Frontend operator experience design
- Data engineering and event modeling

## Core human roles

### Product owner

- Defines outcomes, capital constraints, and acceptable automation.

### Software architect

- Owns service boundaries, contracts, NFRs, and simplification.

### Delivery lead

- Owns sequencing, scope, release readiness, and feedback loops.

### Quant or strategy lead

- Owns hypotheses, backtests, success criteria, and exit rules.

### Risk and compliance lead

- Owns broker policy mapping, approvals, and audit standards.

### Platform engineer

- Owns deployment, observability, reliability, and cloud posture.

## Core AI roles

### AI research partner

- Summarizes markets, extracts signals, and drafts decision support.

### AI architecture partner

- Reviews design, spots complexity, and proposes simpler patterns.

### AI coding partner

- Generates scaffolds, tests, migration steps, and docs.

### AI review partner

- Performs gap analysis, threat review, and quality checks.

## Token ownership

Every role must consider token cost, especially for:

- Prompt size
- Call frequency
- Model choice
- Response verbosity
- Repeated summarization
