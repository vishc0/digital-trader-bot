# Strategy Primer

## Strategy principles

- Start simple.
- Use liquid instruments.
- Prefer explainable entry and exit logic.
- Track regime fit and drift.
- Require measurable expected value.

## Initial strategy set

### 1. Mean reversion

- Entry: oversold conditions in strong liquidity names.
- Exit: move back to mean, stop-loss, or time stop.

### 2. Trend following

- Entry: confirmed momentum with broad market support.
- Exit: trend break, trailing stop, or weakening breadth.

### 3. Portfolio maintenance

- Rebalance concentration risk.
- Trim outsized winners when thesis weakens.
- Exit low-conviction laggards when better use of capital exists.

### 4. Earnings and event caution

- Reduce size before high-volatility events unless the event strategy is explicit.

## Strategy governance

- Each strategy needs hypothesis, entry, exit, risk limit, and observability.
- Each strategy needs a paper-trade evaluation window.
- AI may help rank candidates, but enforcement stays deterministic.

## Compliance note

The platform should help avoid accidental day-trading or broker-policy breaches, not evade them.
