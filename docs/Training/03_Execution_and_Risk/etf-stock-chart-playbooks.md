# ETF and Stock Chart Playbooks

This file translates concepts into actionable chart playbooks.

## Playbook 1: ETF Trend Pullback Continuation

### Conditions

- Daily uptrend (HH/HL).
- Pullback into daily support or 20/50 EMA confluence.
- 1h trigger candle shows rejection + bullish close.

### Execution

- Entry: break of trigger high.
- Stop: below pullback swing low.
- Target: prior high then extension.

### Invalid if

- 1h closes below pullback low with momentum.

## Playbook 2: Range Breakout and Retest (Stock)

### Conditions

- Clear multi-day range.
- Breakout candle closes above range with strong volume.
- Retest holds with bullish rejection.

### Execution

- Entry: retest confirmation.
- Stop: below retest low.
- Target: measured move from range height.

## Playbook 3: Failed Breakout Reversal

### Conditions

- Break above resistance fails and closes back in range.
- Follow-through bearish candle confirms failure.

### Execution

- Entry: break below failure candle low.
- Stop: above failure wick.
- Target: range midpoint then opposite boundary.

## Playbook 4: Gap-and-Go (High Relative Volume)

### Conditions

- Stock gaps up above prior resistance.
- First pullback holds above opening range low.
- Volume stays elevated.

### Execution

- Entry: break of opening range high or pullback rejection.
- Stop: below pullback low.
- Management: scale out into extension candles.

## Playbook 5: Exhaustion Reversal at Weekly Level

### Conditions

- Extended move into weekly resistance/support.
- Rejection candle with high volume.
- Lower timeframe structure break against prior impulse.

### Execution

- Entry on confirmation break.
- Stop beyond rejection wick.
- Target nearest opposing zone.

## ETF-Specific Notes

- Major ETFs often respect VWAP and key index levels intraday.
- Correlation to index futures can improve timing.
- Trend days in ETFs can sustain longer than single names.

## Single-Stock Notes

- Earnings and news can distort normal chart behavior.
- Smaller-cap names may have higher slippage and fakeouts.
- Use stricter liquidity filters for execution reliability.

## Playbook Scoring (0-2 each)

Score each trade before entry:

- Structure alignment
- Level quality
- Candlestick trigger quality
- Volume support
- Risk/reward quality

Trade only if score >= 7/10.

## Final Rule

A playbook is not complete until it has:

- Clear entry
- Clear invalidation
- Clear management
- Post-trade review template
