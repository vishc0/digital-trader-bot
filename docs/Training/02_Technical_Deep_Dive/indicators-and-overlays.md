Indicators should clarify price action, not replace it.

## Primary Indicator Stack (Minimal, Practical)

- 20 EMA: short-term trend rhythm.
- 50 EMA: intermediate trend context.
- 200 EMA: long-term directional filter.
- VWAP (intraday): fair value anchor for session participants.
- RSI (14): momentum and exhaustion context.
- MACD: trend-momentum phase shifts.
- ATR: volatility and stop-distance planning.

## How to Use Moving Averages

### Trend Filter

- Price above rising 20/50/200 EMA stack: bullish environment.
- Price below falling stack: bearish environment.

### Pullback Context

- In strong uptrends, pullbacks to 20/50 EMA can offer continuation setups.
- In weak trends, MA touches fail more often.

## VWAP Use Cases

- Above VWAP with higher highs: intraday bullish bias.
- Reclaim of VWAP after opening weakness: potential trend shift.
- Rejection at VWAP in downtrend: continuation short candidate.

## RSI and Momentum

- RSI > 50 supports bullish momentum environment.
- RSI < 50 supports bearish momentum environment.
- Overbought/oversold alone is not a reversal signal.

## MACD Practical Read

- MACD line cross with price structure break can improve timing.
- Histogram contraction after trend run can warn of momentum slowdown.

## ATR for Risk

- Use ATR to avoid stops that are too tight in high-vol conditions.
- Example: stop beyond structure + 0.5 to 1.0 ATR buffer.

## Scenario 1: EMA + Candlestick Continuation

- ETF in daily uptrend above 50 EMA.
- 1h pullback to 20 EMA prints bullish engulfing.
- RSI holds above 50.
- Setup quality improves via alignment.

## Scenario 2: Indicator Conflict (No Trade)

- Price prints bullish candle at resistance.
- RSI diverges bearish, volume weak, VWAP rejected.
- Signal conflict suggests pass or smaller risk.

## Common Mistakes

- Using too many indicators with overlapping meaning.
- Taking RSI overbought as automatic short signal.
- Ignoring price structure because indicator “says buy.”

## Expert Rule

Price structure is primary, indicator stack is secondary confirmation.
