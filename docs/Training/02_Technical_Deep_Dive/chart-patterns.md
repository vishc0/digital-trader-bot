# Chart Patterns: Structure, Triggers, and Failure Logic

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Foundations](../01_Chart_Foundations/README.md) | [Execution and Risk](../03_Execution_and_Risk/README.md)

Patterns are recurring structures of crowd behavior, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Core Pattern Families

### Continuation Patterns

- Bull/Bear flag
- Pennant
- Ascending/Descending triangle

### Reversal Patterns

- Double top / double bottom
- Head and shoulders / inverse H&S
- Rounded base and topping formations

### Compression/Expansion Patterns

- Inside bar clusters
- Volatility squeeze breakouts

## Pattern Quality Criteria

A pattern is higher quality when:

- It forms in correct context (trend continuation or key reversal zone).
- Boundaries are clear and repeatedly respected.
- Breakout candle shows expansion and participation.
- Retest behavior confirms acceptance.

## Failure Patterns (Very Important)

Failed patterns create strong opposite opportunities.

Example:

- Ascending triangle breaks upward.
- Price quickly closes back below breakout line.
- Follow-through selling triggers long liquidation.

## Scenario 1: Bull Flag Continuation

- Strong impulse up in ETF.
- Drift lower in tight channel (flag) with lower volume.
- Breakout candle closes above flag on strong volume.
- Entry on break or retest; stop below flag low.

## Scenario 2: Double Top Failure

- Stock tests resistance twice.
- Second top forms weaker momentum and rejection wick.
- Neckline break confirms downside structure.

## Scenario 3: Inverse Head and Shoulders

- Downtrend starts basing.
- Left shoulder, head, right shoulder form near support zone.
- Neckline break with volume gives reversal confirmation.

## Pattern Risk Controls

- Entry must have clear invalidation.
- Target should be based on measured move + structure barriers.
- If breakout stalls immediately, reduce risk or exit.

## Teaching Principle

Do not memorize pattern names only. Learn:

- Context
- Trigger
- Invalidation
- Follow-through quality

That is what makes pattern trading professional.
