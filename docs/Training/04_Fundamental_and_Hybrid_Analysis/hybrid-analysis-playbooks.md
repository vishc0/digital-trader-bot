# Hybrid Analysis Playbooks (Fundamental + Technical)

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Trade Construction](chart-to-trade-construction-framework.md)

These playbooks combine fundamental thesis and technical execution.

## Playbook 1: Fundamental Strength + Technical Pullback

### Conditions

- Improving revenue, margin, and guidance trend.
- Daily uptrend intact.
- Pullback into support with reduced volume.

### Trigger

- Bullish reversal candle on setup timeframe.

### Execution

- Entry on trigger break.
- Stop below pullback low.
- Target prior high then trend extension.

## Playbook 2: Fundamental Deterioration + Breakdown

### Conditions

- Earnings revisions down, margin pressure rising.
- Support breaks on strong volume.

### Trigger

- Retest failure below broken support.

### Execution

- Entry on bearish rejection.
- Stop above retest high.
- Target next demand zone.

## Playbook 3: ETF Macro Regime Rotation

### Conditions

- Macro regime shifts (e.g., falling yields).
- Relative strength rotates to growth/tech ETF.

### Trigger

- ETF breakout with breadth support.

### Execution

- Entry on retest hold.
- Stop below breakout failure level.
- Trail while relative strength remains positive.

## Playbook 4: Catalyst Repricing

### Conditions

- Earnings or policy catalyst resets expectations.
- Gap establishes new structural range.

### Trigger

- Acceptance above/below post-catalyst range boundary.

### Execution

- Trade in direction of acceptance.
- Use tighter risk controls due to volatility expansion.

## Hybrid Scorecard (0-2 each)

- Fundamental bias quality
- Macro regime alignment
- Chart setup quality
- Trigger quality
- Participation confirmation (volume/breadth)
- Risk/reward feasibility

Trade only when score >= 8/12.

## Core Principle

Fundamentals improve selection.
Technicals improve timing.
Risk management preserves edge.
