# Fundamental Analysis for Traders (Stocks and ETFs)

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Technical Deep Dive](../02_Technical_Deep_Dive/README.md)

This module focuses on fundamentals as a directional filter and catalyst framework for chart traders.

## Why Traders Need Fundamentals

Fundamentals help answer:

- What can sustain a trend beyond technical bounce?
- Which names deserve priority in watchlists?
- What events can invalidate chart setups quickly?

## Core Fundamental Topics

1. Growth quality (revenue, margin, cash flow).
2. Profitability and operating leverage.
3. Balance sheet health.
4. Valuation regime.
5. Catalyst calendar (earnings, guidance, policy).

## Fundamental Buckets by Trading Use

### A) Trend Sustainability

- Rising revenue + improving margins + strong guidance often support medium-term trend persistence.

### B) Fragility Risk

- Weak cash flow, high debt, deteriorating margins increase breakdown risk.

### C) Catalyst Risk

- Earnings week, FDA decisions, policy shocks can override chart structures short-term.

## ETF-Specific Fundamental Context

For ETFs, analyze:

- Sector composition and concentration.
- Sensitivity to rates/inflation/growth.
- Fund flows and institutional allocation trends.

## Practical Fundamental Filters for Chart Traders

- Prefer longs where fundamentals are stable/improving.
- Prefer shorts where fundamentals are deteriorating and chart confirms.
- Avoid oversized risk before major scheduled catalysts.

## Scenario 1: Strong Fundamentals + Bullish Chart

- Company shows improving margins and raised guidance.
- Chart forms pullback continuation setup in uptrend.
- Confluence increases confidence for swing continuation.

## Scenario 2: Weak Fundamentals + Failed Breakout

- Revenue misses and margin compression.
- Breakout above resistance fails and closes back in range.
- Fundamental and technical alignment supports downside thesis.

## How to Integrate with Chart Process

Use a 3-layer decision model:

- Layer 1: Fundamental bias (bullish/bearish/neutral).
- Layer 2: Technical setup quality.
- Layer 3: Execution timing and risk.

Trade only when at least two layers strongly align.

## Key Rule

Fundamentals set the map. Charts provide timing. Risk rules protect capital.
