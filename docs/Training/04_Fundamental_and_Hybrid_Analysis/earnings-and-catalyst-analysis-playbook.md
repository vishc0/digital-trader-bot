# Earnings and Catalyst Analysis Playbook

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Chart to Trade Framework](chart-to-trade-construction-framework.md)

Catalysts are volatility engines. This module covers how to plan around them.

## Catalyst Types

- Earnings and guidance
- Product launches
- Regulatory decisions
- M&A announcements
- Analyst upgrades/downgrades
- Management changes

## Pre-Earnings Framework

- Map key chart levels (support, resistance, prior gap zones).
- Estimate expected move (options-implied if available).
- Define no-trade, reduced-size, and post-event plans.

## Post-Earnings Framework

Read four things quickly:

1. Price gap location vs major structure.
2. Volume quality.
3. Follow-through or gap fade.
4. Management guidance tone and revisions.

## Gap Behavior Playbook

- Gap-and-go: holds above opening range and continues.
- Gap-fill: initial move reverses and fills toward prior close.
- Gap-trap: breakout gap fails and reverses through key level.

## Scenario 1: Bullish Earnings Continuation

- Beat + raise guidance.
- Gap above major resistance with high volume.
- Intraday pullback holds above breakout.
- Trend continuation setup quality improves.

## Scenario 2: Good Report, Bad Price Action

- Solid numbers but weak reaction.
- Stock fails to hold gap and closes red.
- Price-action message: expectations were too high.

## Risk Rules Around Catalysts

- Avoid oversized pre-event positions.
- Use smaller size for first post-event setup.
- Require stronger confirmation than normal days.

## Weekly Catalyst Workflow

- Build watchlist of names reporting this week.
- Pre-mark levels and expected scenarios.
- Execute only pre-planned playbooks, not impulsive reactions.
