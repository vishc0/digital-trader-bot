# Valuation and Multiples in Practice

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Fundamental Analysis](fundamental-analysis-for-traders.md)

Valuation helps determine whether upside/downside expectations are already priced in.

## Core Multiples

- P/E (Price to Earnings)
- EV/EBITDA
- Price to Sales
- Price to Free Cash Flow
- PEG (P/E adjusted by growth)

## How Traders Use Valuation

Valuation is not a timing trigger. It is a context filter.

- Rich valuation + weak momentum = vulnerable to sharp repricing.
- Reasonable valuation + improving growth = better continuation backdrop.

## Relative Valuation

Compare company versus:

- Its own historical range.
- Sector peers.
- Market regime (growth vs value environment).

## ETF Valuation Context

For sector ETFs, monitor:

- Aggregate valuation spread vs broad market.
- Sector earnings revisions.
- Positioning extremes and flow sensitivity.

## Scenario 1: Expensive Growth with Momentum Fade

- Stock trades 2 standard deviations above historical P/E.
- Guidance only in-line, chart loses momentum.
- Reversal setups gain quality.

## Scenario 2: Value Re-rating Setup

- Company trading below peer multiples despite improving margins.
- Breakout from long base with strong volume.
- Valuation and technicals align for medium-term upside.

## Practical Rules

- Do not short just because valuation is high.
- Do not buy just because valuation is low.
- Use valuation to rank opportunity quality, then execute via chart structure.

## Watchlist Ranking Model

- Growth trajectory score
- Valuation attractiveness score
- Chart setup score
- Catalyst score

Trade highest composite scores with valid risk structure.
