# Professional Timeframe Combinations Guide

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Chart Foundations](../01_Chart_Foundations/README.md) | [Execution and Risk](../03_Execution_and_Risk/README.md)

This guide defines the most used timeframe stacks by professional traders for stocks and ETFs.

## Principle: Anchor -> Setup -> Trigger

Use three layers:

1. Anchor timeframe: trend and major levels.
2. Setup timeframe: where the pattern forms.
3. Trigger timeframe: precise entry and risk placement.

## Best Combinations by Style

### 1) Position Trading (days to weeks)

- Anchor: Weekly
- Setup: Daily
- Trigger: 4H or 1H

Why this works:

- Weekly reduces noise and aligns with institutional swing structure.
- Daily gives practical setup resolution.
- 4H/1H gives risk-efficient timing.

### 2) Swing Trading (2-10 days)

- Anchor: Daily
- Setup: 4H
- Trigger: 1H or 15M

Why this works:

- Daily defines dominant structure.
- 4H captures pullback/base patterns.
- 1H/15M improves entry precision without micro-noise.

### 3) Intraday Trading (minutes to hours)

- Anchor: Daily and 4H
- Setup: 1H and 15M
- Trigger: 5M and 1M (only for execution)

Why this works:

- Prevents overreacting to 1M noise.
- Keeps entries aligned with higher-timeframe bias.

### 4) ETF Index Momentum Days

- Anchor: Daily
- Setup: 30M/15M
- Trigger: 5M

Why this works:

- ETFs often trend around session VWAP and opening range levels.
- 5M is fast enough for execution but stable enough to avoid excessive noise.

## What Not to Do

- Weekly anchor with 1M trigger for beginners.
- Making decisions only on 1M chart without daily context.
- Switching timeframes randomly after entry.

## Timeframe Pairing Cheatsheet

- Weekly -> Daily -> 4H: macro swing and position ideas.
- Daily -> 4H -> 1H: standard professional swing stack.
- Daily/4H -> 15M -> 5M: high-quality intraday alignment.
- Daily/1H -> 5M -> 1M: experienced intraday execution only.

## Session Timing (US Equities/ETFs)

Most actionable chart behavior appears in:

- First 60-90 minutes after open.
- Last 60 minutes before close.

Midday often has lower quality follow-through.

## Example: Daily-4H-1H Swing Workflow

- Daily: identify uptrend and support zone.
- 4H: wait for pullback + compression.
- 1H: enter on bullish trigger candle above local structure.

Stop goes below 1H invalidation, while target references 4H/daily structure.

## Core Rule

Your timeframe stack should match your intended holding period. If these are mismatched, execution quality drops fast.
