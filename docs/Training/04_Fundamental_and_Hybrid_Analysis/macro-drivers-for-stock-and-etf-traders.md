# Macro Drivers for Stock and ETF Traders

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [ETF Framework](etf-fundamental-framework.md)

Macro does not replace technicals; it changes the probability distribution of outcomes.

## The Main Macro Variables

- Interest rates and yield curve
- Inflation trend
- Growth expectations
- Liquidity conditions
- USD direction
- Energy and commodity shocks

## Why Macro Matters for Charts

A perfect chart setup can fail in hostile macro conditions.

Examples:

- Hawkish surprise can break growth-stock support zones quickly.
- Falling yields can accelerate long-duration equity breakouts.

## Regime Mapping

Build simple regime labels:

- Risk-on growth
- Defensive risk-off
- Inflation scare
- Disinflation relief

Then align instrument selection accordingly.

## Sector Sensitivity (Simplified)

- Tech/growth: often sensitive to real yields.
- Financials: sensitive to curve shape and credit cycle.
- Energy/materials: sensitive to commodity trends.
- Utilities/staples: defensive behavior in risk-off phases.

## Scenario 1: Disinflation + Falling Yields

- Growth ETFs regain relative strength.
- Breakout pullback setups in tech names improve.

## Scenario 2: Inflation Re-acceleration Shock

- Rate-sensitive sectors weaken.
- Defensive and commodity-linked groups outperform.

## Macro Event Calendar for Traders

Track:

- CPI/PCE
- FOMC decisions
- Payrolls
- GDP surprises
- Major geopolitical events

Reduce size around high-volatility macro events unless setup is exceptional.

## Integration Rule

Macro chooses where to fish.
Charts choose when to fish.
Risk rules decide how much to fish.
