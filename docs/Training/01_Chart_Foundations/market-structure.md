# Market Structure for ETF and Stock Chart Trading

[Training Hub](../README.md) | [Section Index](./README.md) | [Technical Deep Dive](../02_Technical_Deep_Dive/README.md)

Market structure is the backbone of chart trading. Candles are signals; structure is context.

## Why Structure Matters

Without structure, patterns become random. With structure, you can classify price behavior into:

- Trend (up or down)
- Range (balance)
- Transition (possible regime change)

## Core Building Blocks

- Swing high: a local peak where price turns down.
- Swing low: a local trough where price turns up.
- Higher high (HH), higher low (HL): bullish structure.
- Lower high (LH), lower low (LL): bearish structure.

## Three Structure States

### 1) Uptrend

- Sequence of HH + HL.
- Pullbacks hold above prior key lows.
- Best chart behavior: buy pullbacks into demand.

### 2) Downtrend

- Sequence of LH + LL.
- Rallies fail below prior key highs.
- Best chart behavior: sell rallies into supply.

### 3) Range

- High and low boundaries repeatedly respected.
- Mean reversion dominates until true breakout.

## Break of Structure (BOS) vs Change of Character (CHOCH)

- BOS: continuation break in direction of prevailing trend.
- CHOCH: break against prevailing trend, warning of transition.

Example:

- Market in uptrend (HH/HL).
- Price breaks below important HL and fails to reclaim.
- This can mark CHOCH and begin bearish transition.

## ETF/Stock Practical Structure Rules

- Use daily chart to map primary structure.
- Use 1h or 4h to locate active swing zone.
- Use 5m/15m only for entry timing.

## Scenario 1: Bullish Structure Continuation

- Daily uptrend, ETF pulls back to prior breakout level.
- 1h chart forms higher low + bullish engulfing.
- Entry after trigger break, stop below HL, target prior high then extension.

## Scenario 2: Range Fakeout

- Stock breaks range high intraday.
- Candle closes back inside range.
- Next candle rejects high again.
- Interpretation: failed breakout, likely move to range midpoint or opposite boundary.

## Structure Mistakes to Avoid

- Treating every minor pivot as structural swing.
- Trading lower timeframe against dominant daily structure.
- Forcing trend trades inside obvious ranges.

## Structure Checklist

Before trade:

1. What structure state am I in?
2. Is this continuation or transition attempt?
3. Where is invalidation relative to key swing?
4. Is reward enough before next structure barrier?

Master structure first; everything else becomes clearer.
