Multi-timeframe analysis aligns context, setup, and timing.

## The Three-Layer Framework

1. Higher timeframe (HTF): bias and structure map.
2. Setup timeframe (STF): where trade idea forms.
3. Entry timeframe (ETF): precise trigger and risk placement.

Example stack:

- HTF: Daily
- STF: 1h or 4h
- ETF: 5m or 15m

## Workflow

### Step 1: HTF Bias

- Identify trend/range state.
- Mark major support/resistance and recent swing points.

### Step 2: STF Setup

- Wait for price to reach decision zone.
- Monitor pattern quality (pullback, base, breakout, rejection).

### Step 3: ETF Trigger

- Enter only on clear trigger candle and invalidation point.

## Alignment Rules

- Best continuation trades occur when all three timeframes align.
- Counter-trend trades require stronger confirmation and smaller size.

## Scenario 1: Aligned Long

- Daily uptrend.
- 1h pullback into support.
- 15m bullish engulfing with volume pickup.
- Trade has layered confluence.

## Scenario 2: Misaligned Short

- Daily strong uptrend.
- 5m bearish pattern appears intraday.
- Without HTF resistance or structure break, short is lower quality.

## Common Mistakes

- Entering on 1-5m noise without HTF context.
- Letting lower-timeframe fear override higher-timeframe plan.
- Ignoring daily key levels while scalping intraday.

## Practical Checklist

Before entry:

1. HTF bias clear?
2. STF setup at meaningful zone?
3. ETF trigger confirmed?
4. Stop and target fit structure?

No alignment, no trade.
